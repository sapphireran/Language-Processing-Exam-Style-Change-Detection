I swear weeknight pasta's the only dinner I still bother with. It's stupidly forgiving! You boil water, you throw in way too much salt, and you pretend you measured the noodles. Tonight I used whatever veg was about to die in the fridge: a wrinkly pepper, half an onion, and some spinach that had opinions. Garlic went in last because I always burn it if I get cocky. The pasta sauce was just tomatoes, chili flakes, and a reckless splash of the pasta water. It wasn't restaurant food. It was the kind of dinner you eat standing up.

Leftovers hit different when you refuse to reheat the pasta properly. I ate it cold from the container and I'm not sorry! Extra oil, more cheese than is socially acceptable, and suddenly it's a new pasta dinner. If you want to feel fancy, toast the stale bread and rub it with the leftover garlic. That's the entire leftover recipe. Nobody needs a lecture about emulsification at 11pm.

Municipal street design is often treated as a purely engineering problem; yet the distribution of sidewalk shade, pedestrian seating, and crossing time has measurable effects on who can comfortably use a corridor. When a carriageway is widened at the expense of the sidewalk, the implicit priority is vehicular throughput rather than pedestrian dwell time. A more careful brief would treat pedestrian delay as a cost with the same status as vehicle delay. Evidence from several mid-sized cities suggests that narrower lanes, if paired with frequent crossings, do not produce the congestion catastrophe that is routinely predicted.

Implementation of a sidewalk redesign, however, is constrained by maintenance budgets and by the political salience of corridor parking. A curb that is reallocated to a parklet in April can be recaptured by November if surrounding businesses perceive a loss of short-stay parking spaces. Therefore any pedestrian redesign should publish a monitoring plan before construction begins: counts of walking, lingering, and sidewalk use, collected on a seasonal cycle. Without that public ledger, the first complaint about parking is able to stand in for the whole evaluation of the corridor.
