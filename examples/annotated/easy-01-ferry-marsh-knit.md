# Annotation: easy-01 ferry / marsh / knit

Gold: `[0, 1, 0, 1, 0]` — three authors, two joins.

## P1–P2 (gold 0) ferry commuter

First person, contractions (`don't`, `I'm` energy even when the
surface is `I don't`), short sentences, a complaint about the 6:40
boat. Topic is the harbour commute. Both paragraphs share `I`,
jacket/hands/deck residue, and a punchy cadence. A detector that
flags this pair is reacting to length or to the second paragraph's
slightly higher `you`.

## P2–P3 (gold 1) commuter → hydrologist

This is the easy-band cheat code: **author and topic move together**.
Function words shift from `I`/`don't` to `of`/`the`/`these`. Sentence
length roughly doubles. Academic markers (`empirical`, `indicate`,
`findings`) appear. Formality jump should dominate the ensemble.
Topic Jaccard will also be high — refuse to call that style.

## P3–P4 (gold 0) two hydrology paragraphs

Same passive, same hedges, same marsh hydroperiod. `However` in P4 is
still the same voice, not a new author. If character 3-grams twitch
because `gelatine` is absent, ignore them; the closed class is
stable.

## P4–P5 (gold 1) hydrologist → knitting instructor

Second-person imperatives (`Cast on`, `Do not`, `Measure`). The
content is wool, not inundation. Again, topic and style move. The
oral point: name the *style* cues (imperatives, `you`, short
commands) before you mention that the topic changed.

## P5–P6 (gold 0) two knitting paragraphs

Still instructions. Bind-off, mattress stitch, the same `you`. A
false positive here is usually sentence-length shock from a longer
closing sentence.

## What to say if asked "is this realistic?"

No. Easy PAN data is Reddit, not ferry diaries. The *contract* is
realistic: adjacent bits, topic allowed to leak. The prose is a
whiteboard.
