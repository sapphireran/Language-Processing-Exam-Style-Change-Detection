# Annotation: medium-04 bouldering gym

Gold: `[0, 1, 0, 1, 0]` — one gym, three registers.

## Why this is medium

Every paragraph is about the same cave slab, the same green problem,
the same mats. Content-word Jaccard should not be a free lunch.
What changes is **who is allowed to speak**:

- P1–P2: forum climber. `I'm`, `yeah`, `whatever`, sandwich, film
  etiquette as a gripe.
- P3–P4: safety officer. Imperatives, `Do not`, incident logs, hex
  keys. Almost no first person.
- P5–P6: training log. First person again, but clipped (`I'm logging
  this so I don't rewrite the week`), times, tape, the last bus.

## P2–P3 (gold 1)

The join the baseline is built to catch: diary → instructions.
Formality jumps, first person drops, `you` as complaint is replaced
by `Inspect` as a verb of work.

## P4–P5 (gold 1)

Instructions → log. Harder, because both voices know the wall. The
log brings `I` back and throws away `shall`-like completeness.
If the detector misses this one, say so: close registers on a shared
topic are the medium-band lesson.

## What not to do

Do not cluster paragraphs 1, 2, 5, 6 as "the same author" just
because they use `I`. The task asks about *adjacent* joins. A
returning first-person voice after the safety officer is still a `1`.
