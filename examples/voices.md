# House voices, with a line you can hear

**stall** — "I'm heading home once the pyrometer drops under two hundred."

**notice** — "The kiln shall remain locked until the recorded temperature falls below ninety degrees Celsius."

**notebook** — "We recorded partials of the tenor after the second scrape and the hum note remained a little sharp."

**letter** — "You would have liked the size of the stone this morning; I floated a comb that finally behaved."

**minutes** — "It was resolved that visiting yachts shall radio the harbour office before entering the cut."

**chat** — "yeah I'm sliding the interview because the tape's a mess and I don't have a clean in-point."

Pair stall with notice for an easy fold. Pair notebook with notebook
for a hard miss. Pair stall with stall across three topics for a
control. Pair chat with chat if you want the detector to embarrass
itself.
