You'll hear the kiln change its voice when the charge is nearly done. Don't trust the first quiet; you can wait a bit and listen again. Your poker will tell you more than the smoke. Maybe give it one more hour?

You can rake a path for the next load now. Don't leave the barrow where you'll trip. If your eyes are full of dust you'll miss the crack in the eye. Sort of circle it once before you go.

We ought to admit that the millrace is a public sentence written in water. One therefore reads the debris as clauses, not as waste. However local the repair, the grammar belongs to the whole cut. This does not flatter the owner.

Our maps still stop at the wheel. We should draw the race back to the first hatch. Therefore a walk in the rain is not a hobby. It is the cheapest way to keep the argument honest.
