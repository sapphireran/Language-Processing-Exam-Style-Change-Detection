I spent the afternoon in the tide pool behind the harbour wall.
I counted sixteen anemones and I lost a boot to the weed.
The water was so clear I could see my own knees.
I think the pool is smaller than it was last April.
We present a four-year survey of intertidal pools which demonstrates, however, a 12 percent reduction in mean depth towards the harbour wall.
Anemone counts remain stable whilst algal cover has increased; the pattern is therefore interpreted as sedimentation rather than as harvest pressure.
Implications for municipal dredging policy are discussed.
