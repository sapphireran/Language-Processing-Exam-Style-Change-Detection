The allotment committee will not pay for a new tap. That is the sentence I left with, and I have been turning it over like a stone that might have a better side. We were promised a vote. We received a shrug dressed as a minute.

The minute records that a replacement tap was discussed and that funds are presently committed to the fence. No vote is minuted because no motion was put. Members who wish to reopen the matter should submit an item by Thursday noon. Informal conversations at the water butt do not constitute a motion.

I know how minutes work. I have written them. I also know when a room decides not to hear a hose that has been leaking since February. If the fence is the hill, say that with your mouth. Do not hide behind noon on Thursday.

Submitted items will be circulated. The leaking hose is acknowledged. The tap remains unbudgeted. This is not a personal refusal. It is the state of the account as of this evening.
