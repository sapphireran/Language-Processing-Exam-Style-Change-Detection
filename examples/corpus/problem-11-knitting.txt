Cast on 96 stitches. Join in the round, being careful not to twist. Work 2x2 rib for 6 cm. Switch to stockinette. Increase 8 stitches evenly on the next round. Place a marker at the start of round and a second marker after 48 stitches to mark the sides.

Continue until the body measures 28 cm from the cast-on edge, or to taste if you are longer in the rib than I am. Begin raglan decreases: k2tog before each marker and ssk after, every other round, eight times. Separate sleeves on scrap yarn. The remaining stitches are the yoke, which is not a metaphor.

I started this sweater in a January that would not end. The wool smelled like a shop that had decided to be kind to me. I kept the pattern in a browser tab I refused to bookmark, as if bookmarking would make the sleeves real and therefore fail.

When the raglan math went wrong I did not frog it. I invented a saddle and told people it was on purpose. They believed me because a finished object is an argument. I still have the scrap yarn from the sleeves in a jar, which is not inventory, just evidence.
