The jacket was eight euro and still had a cinema ticket in the pocket from a film I have been meaning to pretend I saw. I bought it because the lining was intact and because the rain had started in that vertical way that makes umbrellas look theoretical. I looked, I swear, like a person who owns more than one chair.

Has anyone else noticed that the good racks are the ones behind the baby clothes? Why do they hide the wool? Are we being trained? If you find a wool skirt with functional pockets I will trade you a plant I have not yet killed. What size is a size that is lying?

Intake log, Saturday. 14 bags. 3 rejected for mould. Winter rail over capacity; overflow on table 2. Pricing: jackets 8–15 unless branded, in which case ask Mara. Cinema tickets in pockets to be discarded, not displayed. Volunteer notes are not inventory.

I went back for buttons and left with a novel that smells like a bus. The jacket still feels like a decision I can defend. The plant remains alive, which is more than I promised the person with the skirt.
