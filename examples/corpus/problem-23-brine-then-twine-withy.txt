Cutting shall follow the marked stools only. A rod thinner than the gauge must be left. Bundles shall be tied with withy, not with twine from the shop. The inspector shall count the stools before and after.

Peelings shall remain on the bed. No fire shall be lit among the stools. The gate must be shut against stock. The date of cut shall be painted on the first post.

You'll want to cut on a dull day, maybe after a bit of rain so the bark gives. Don't strip a stool bald; you can leave a few rods to drink. Your knife stays cleaner if you wipe it. Can you feel the green still in there?

You might sort the crooked ones now. Don't throw them in the ditch; you'll want them for ties. If your hands are cold you'll nick the good rods. Sort of breathe on them and keep the pile in the shade.
