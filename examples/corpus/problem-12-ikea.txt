Insert A into the holes along the long edge of panel 1. Attach panel 2. Turn B clockwise until it grips. If B spins freely, C is not in the cam. Reverse and try again. Do not invent a fourth hole. The extra hole you are looking at is for a hinge in another language.

The wardrobe is a small essay about optimism. We buy a room in a flat pack because we would like the future to arrive with an Allen key and a numbering scheme. The failure mode is not the missing screw. It is the afternoon in which two people discover they do not share a definition of "level".

I assembled mine against a radiator I had forgotten about. The back panel bowed like a guilty secret. I still hang shirts in it. They lean. I lean with them and call it a lived-in aesthetic, which is what you call a cam lock you tightened too soon.
