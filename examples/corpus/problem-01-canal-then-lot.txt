I walked the towpath before the first hire boat stirred.
I counted eight lock gates and I wrote the numbers on my cuff.
The bottom gate leaked a thin silver thread onto the cill.
I ate the last plum on the coping stone and watched a moorhen.
Lot 14: oak lock-gate leaf, 3.2 m by 0.48 m; iron strap hinges, c. 1891.
The object retains original pintles; surface chloride 12 g/kg.
A later paint skin, alkyd, 0.3 mm, covers the landward face only.
