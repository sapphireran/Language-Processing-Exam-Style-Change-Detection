Dearest Nia, do you remember the pond behind the school when the reeds wore ice like glass sleeves!
I still see your red mittens on the rail, and I remain quite sure you dared me first.
We laughed so hard the dog came running, and I remain sure the whole village heard us!
The committee subsequently noted that the recreation pond shall be closed whenever ice thickness is recorded below 120 mm.
Access gates are to remain locked; however, a warden may admit maintenance staff between 08:00 and 09:00.
Accordingly, public notices shall be posted at both stiles and at the parish hall.
