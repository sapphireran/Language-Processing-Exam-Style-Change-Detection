You'll want to test the face with your heel, maybe more than once if it looks a bit shiny. Don't step where it gives. You can lay the next row on the drier strip. Can you hear the suck under there?

You might turn the early turves this afternoon. Don't stack them wet; you'll get a sulky heap. If your back is loud you'll know to stop. Sort of count a smaller pile and leave.

You'll see a skin if you wait a bit after the rake. Don't chase the last crystals into the dirt. You can leave a line for tomorrow. Maybe cover the corner the gulls like?

You'll walk the east table the same way. Don't scrape the board if it is a bit damp. You can leave the last skin. Maybe that is enough for the light you have?
