I found the amber after the tide pulled back from the shingle.
I rinsed it in the thermos cup and I held it to the sun.
A trapped gnat sat inside like a comma in honey.
I put it in the tobacco tin with the sea glass.
Specimen A-441: Baltic succinite, 18.4 g; inclusion Diptera, incomplete.
The object is drilled at 2.1 mm for a later thong; polish is modern.
Display mount: brass pin, 40 mm; label in 8-point Bembo.
