I take the Helsingør ferry when I want a border that still feels like a chore rather than a performance.
The coffee is a performance anyway, but the water is not trying to be anything except wide.
I stand on the cold side and I watch Sweden thicken without getting romantic about nations.
If the wind is ugly I sit inside and I listen to people negotiate the price of a pastry in two languages.
I have taken this crossing enough times that the announcement is a texture, not information.
On the way back I always think I will work and I always watch the wake instead.
That is not a style change; that is the same person failing the same plan.
I write it down so a detector has a long single-author document that still contains a harbour, a café, and a return.
The topics move; the habits do not: first person, short clauses, a little stubbornness about romance.
When the ramp drops I walk off at the same pace I walked on.
