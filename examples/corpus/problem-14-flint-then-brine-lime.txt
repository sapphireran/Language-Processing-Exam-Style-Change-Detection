Charge 1.6 t stone, 0.4 t culm. Lit 05:05. Draught good. Eye colour: rose. Wind 9 kn, SW.

Draw 1 at 14:20, 180 kg. Fines 12 percent. Core white. Unburnt: 3 pieces. Banked 16:40.

A kiln shall not be drawn until the core is white throughout. Stone must be of the named bed. Culm shall be stored dry. The inspector shall weigh the first draw and shall record the unburnt count.

The eye shall be kept clear of clinker. No person shall stand in the fume. Tools must be quenched before they are hung. The yard gate shall be locked after the last barrow.
