I cut with the wind at my back. I don't fight a gust on the moss. I'll lay the turves to dry in little roofs and I'll turn them when I hear them skin. So I keep my tea in the tin.

I sank to the knee near the old pit. I don't go that way twice. I'll mark it with a stick and I'll tell the boy. Then I rinse the spade and I sit on the dry bank.

Pit 4, spit 3. Depth 0.9 m. Water 0.2 m at face. Turves out: 240. Wind 11 kn, W. Break 10:15.
