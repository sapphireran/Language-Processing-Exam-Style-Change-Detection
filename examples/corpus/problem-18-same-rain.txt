The rain arrived as a vertical grey and I let it have the street without bargaining.
I stood under the bakery awning and counted the seconds between gusts as if counting were a roof.
When it eased I walked home with my sleeves dark and I did not try to make the walk symbolic.
Precipitation 4 mm between 16:00 and 17:00, wind SSE 8 m/s, visibility reduced but not to fog criteria.
Temperature held at 11 C; no thunder was recorded at the city station.
Relative humidity was not logged at the balcony; the city series is the reference.
No thunder was heard from this address either, which agrees with the station note.
The hour is the same hour; the units are millimetres, metres per second, and degrees.
I file the balcony as uninstrumented and I leave the official millimetres to the station.
