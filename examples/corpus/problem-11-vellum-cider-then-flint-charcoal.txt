We ought to talk about cider as a pressure, not as a festival. One therefore watches the cloths, the hair, and the first run as a single claim about patience. However sweet the smell, the argument is in the pomace. This does not need a speech at the orchard gate.

Our habit is to praise the vintage and to forget the press. We should reverse that. Therefore a stained screw is more interesting than a label. One does not make a year by naming it.
