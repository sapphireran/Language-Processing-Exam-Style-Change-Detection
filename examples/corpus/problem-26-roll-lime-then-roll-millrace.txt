The committee shall receive the kiln log each Friday. Members were informed that the last charge was drawn late. It was resolved that the next burn must start at dawn. The clerk shall keep the culm weights.

The eye was reported dirty. The committee must order a clearing before the next charge. It was noted that two barrows were left in the rain. Members shall not leave tools in the yard.

The committee shall walk the race after the next frost. Members were shown the boards that were sprung. It was resolved that those boards must be replaced. The clerk shall write to the miller.

The hatch gear was reported stiff. The committee must obtain grease and a spare pin. It was agreed that the willow shall be cut back. Members shall not stand on the ice shelf.
