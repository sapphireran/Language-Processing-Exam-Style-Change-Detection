The balcony tomato has decided to live. I did not expect that after May. I water it from a jar because the watering can lives at the allotment, and I pinch the side shoots when I remember, which is not a system so much as a series of apologies the plant keeps accepting.

It smells like a greenhouse even though it is just a pot and a stubbornness. Yesterday I ate one fruit standing up, over the sink, like a person who has not earned a plate. The seeds stuck to my wrist. I left them there for a minute as evidence.

The train sat outside the station long enough for people to start performing patience. I counted four coughs and one very polite swear. When we finally moved, the announcement blamed "a person on the track earlier", which is a sentence that always feels unfinished.

I got home late and the tomato was still there, which is a low bar and the only one I cleared. I should pack a book for the next delay. I packed a tomato instead, in my head, which does not help on the platform.
