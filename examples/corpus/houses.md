# House voices

These six voices are the only authors in the toy bank. They are not
real people and they are not scraped. Each house is a closed-class
habit I can keep still while I rotate the topic.

The detector is not allowed to read the house names. They exist so I
can write documents on purpose, and so I can explain a miss in the
oral without waving at "the vibe".

## Caliper

Third person, past tense, quantities, no contractions. Sentences sit
around eighteen words. Favourite verbs: *recorded*, *noted*, *filed*,
*fitted*, *entered*. Likes a tin, a card, a second copy. Rarely says
*I* or *you*.

**Closed-class tell:** high *the* / *was* / *were*, digit rate, almost
no questions.

## Placard

Second person, short sentences, mild imperative. Talks as if the
reader is already holding the object. Favourite words: *you*, *your*,
*will*, *today*, *ready*. No hedges. No *shall*.

**Closed-class tell:** *you* / *your*, short mean sentence length,
almost no *I*.

## Seminar

First person plural plus hedges. Long sentences that refuse to land
until a caveat has been paid. Favourite moves: *we might*, *however*,
*this does not imply*, *the useful question*. Nominalisations
(*measurement*, *variation*, *correction*).

**Closed-class tell:** *we*, *however*, *may* / *might*, long
sentences, *this*.

## Pocket

First person singular, sensory nouns, dashes. Will admit to feeling
foolish. Keeps an hour, a smell, a lock that sticks. Not a joke voice
and not a tweet — a notebook.

**Closed-class tell:** *I* / *my*, dash rate, medium sentences,
almost no *shall*.

## Statute

Deontic. *Shall* and *must*, *any* and *such*, *provided that*. No
contractions. No *I*. Nested clauses. A spent nib is retained for
thirty days whether or not anyone wants it.

**Closed-class tell:** *shall* / *must* / *any* / *such*, long
sentences, zero contractions.

## Bench

Spoken, contracted, slightly defensive. *Anyway*, *honestly*, a
question that is not a request for data. Will mention a note from
last time. Not slang for its own sake — just the person who actually
opened the well.

**Closed-class tell:** contraction rate, *anyway* / *honestly*,
questions, *I* without the dash habit of Pocket.

## What I refuse to put in the vector

Topic nouns (*stilling well*, *xenon*, *stop-log*, *QSL*) are how I
*write* the bank, not how I *score* it. If Caliper writes about a
cinema booth and then a river gauge, the nouns move and the function
words should not. That is the whole point of the trap documents.
