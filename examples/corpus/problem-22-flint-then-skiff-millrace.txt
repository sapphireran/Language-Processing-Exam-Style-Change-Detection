Hatch 1: 0.35 m open. Rack debris 4 kg. Ice shelf 8 cm on north bank. Wheel rpm 12. Leak at launder joint: drip.

Silt 0.22 m at bend 3. Willow lean 20 deg. Board 7 sprung. Temp 1 C. Walk time 18 min.

I poke the ice with a pole and I listen. I don't stand on it. I'll clear the rack with the long hook and I'll pile the sticks on the bank. So I keep my boots on the boards.

I know the sprung board by the sound. I don't cross it loaded. I'll wedge it for today and I'll bring a new one tomorrow. Then I shut the hatch a little and I watch the wheel.
