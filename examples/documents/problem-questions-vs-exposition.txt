A language model assigns a probability to every string in the language and is typically estimated from corpus counts.
Smoothing redistributes probability mass from seen n-grams to unseen ones so that no test string is assigned zero.
Local n-gram context cannot encode unbounded syntactic agreement; a latent or neural state is therefore required.
