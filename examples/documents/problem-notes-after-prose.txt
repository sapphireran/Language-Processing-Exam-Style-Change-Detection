A projective dependency tree can be encoded as a sequence of stack-and-buffer transitions.
An oracle then maps each gold tree onto the unique transition sequence that reconstructs it.
A classifier is trained to imitate that oracle given a representation of the current configuration.
Character-level LSTMs recover most out-of-vocabulary losses on the development set.
trans: shift | left-arc | right-arc
oracle: gold tree -> unique path
feat: stack[0], buffer[0], POS, dist
dev: +1.4 UAS with char-LSTM
