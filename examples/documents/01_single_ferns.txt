I keep two Boston ferns on the kitchen shelf and I water them when the moss on top looks tired, not on a calendar. If I stick to a Sunday ritual I drown them, so I just poke the soil and decide.

They dropped a few fronds in March and I almost threw the pot out. Then I moved them one metre back from the radiator and they calmed down. That is the whole method. No mister, no plant app.

The hanging one in the bathroom is greedier. Steam from the shower seems to make it smug. I still lose a leaf now and then and I do not take it personally.
