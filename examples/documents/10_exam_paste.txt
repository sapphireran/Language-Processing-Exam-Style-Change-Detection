Style change detection is the task of finding positions in a document where the author switches. In the paragraph-level version, each paragraph has one author, and the system emits a binary label for every pair of neighbours.

Style-change detection (also known as multi-author writing style analysis) is an intrinsic authorship problem in which a system must identify the positions at which authorship changes within a single document, typically at paragraph boundaries, without access to a reference corpus of candidate authors.
