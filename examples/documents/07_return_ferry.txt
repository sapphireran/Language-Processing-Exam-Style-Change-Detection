The morning crossing was flat enough that I wrote in the notebook without bracing the page. A man sold coffee from a thermos that had seen better decades. I bought one anyway.

Rooms were clean, breakfast started at seven, parking was included. Staff responded to a noise complaint within fifteen minutes. Would stay again if the rate holds.

I slept on the return with my head on the rucksack and missed the announcement for the island. Someone tapped my shoulder. Same thermos man. I tipped him the coins I had left.
