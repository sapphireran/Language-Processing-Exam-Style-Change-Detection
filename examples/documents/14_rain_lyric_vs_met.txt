Rain arrived the way a shy guest does, first on the bins, then on the window, then as if it had always been in the room. I let the letter sit unread because the street had become a river of brake lights.

A warm front crossed the Sound overnight. Precipitation subsequently began as light rain, becoming moderate after 02:00; surface winds backed from south-west to south. Visibility therefore fell to four kilometres.

I thought of the last time we waited this out, the kettle clicking, the argument that never quite started. The city sounds smaller in rain. That is not meteorology. That is just what I heard.

Accumulations of 8 to 12 mm are expected by morning, locally higher over the western suburbs. No warnings are in force. The next update will be issued at 06:00.
