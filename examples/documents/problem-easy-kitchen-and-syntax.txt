A constituency tree recursively groups words into nested phrases such as NP and VP.
The resulting structure makes subject–predicate relations explicit for a parser.
Agreement features are subsequently percolated from the head to the phrasal node.
CFG: S -> NP VP
NP -> Det N | N
VP -> V NP | V
