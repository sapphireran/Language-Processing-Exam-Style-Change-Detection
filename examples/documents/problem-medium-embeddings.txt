I keep mixing up count vectors and embeddings, so here is how I remember it.
If two words show up in the same kind of sentence, I expect their vectors to end up close.
That is basically why king minus man plus woman lands near queen, I think.
Distributional semantics represents each word as a real-valued vector derived from co-occurrence statistics.
Geometric regularities in that space recover analogical relations such as gender and tense.
Dimensionality reduction, typically via SVD or a neural objective, therefore yields the final embedding.
