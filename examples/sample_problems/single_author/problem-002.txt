Tuesday I walked to the market because the bike had a flat I did not want to look at. The long way past the canal is slower and I took it anyway. A kid was feeding ducks bread against every sign in the park, and the ducks had strong opinions. I bought too many pears. By the time I got home the bag handle had carved a stripe into my fingers, which felt like a fair tax.

Wednesday I finally patched the tire and immediately found a second hole, which is the kind of joke the week likes to tell. I still rode to the library. The return slot was jammed with somebody else's ambitious stack. I left my novels on the desk and walked out lighter. On the way back the pears were waiting like a small, ticking project.

Thursday the canal path was closed for reasons printed on a sign too faded to trust. I detoured through the lots behind the bakery and came out slightly lost and slightly flour-dusted. That counted as a win. I ate the last pear over the sink and decided the bike could wait until Saturday. It will not wait until Saturday. I already know that, and I am going to try to forget until Friday night.
