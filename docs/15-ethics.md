# Ethics (the short page I will not skip)

Style-change detection is sold, in the shared-task blurb, as
a way to find gift authorship, plagiarism without a source,
or a writing-support warning. Those are research stories. A
personal exam lab is not a forensic instrument.

## What this bank is

Fiction. Six invented houses. Thirty short documents I
wrote so I could see my own hinges. A vote rule that I can
do on paper. Nothing here was sampled from a platform. Nothing
here is a student script, a paper, or a workplace memo.

## What I will not say

I will not say the tool "identified an author". It labelled
a hinge on a dialect map I designed. I will not say a low
vote count "clears" a writer. Absence of a bundle is not
innocence; it is "not enough closed-class movement in this
window". I will not offer this as evidence in a misconduct
story.

## Dual use, said plainly

A method that looks for a second hand can be pointed at
whistleblowers, at anonymous notes, at exam scripts. I am
not building a product. I am learning why topic is a cheat
and why accuracy is a liar. If I ever take the method off
the toy bank, the first extra page is still this one.

## Copyright and data

No PAN files are in the repo. No Reddit dumps. The house
sheet is mine. The MIT license on the repo is for the code
and the notes, not a claim that a dialect map is a person.
