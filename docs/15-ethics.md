# Ethics

Style change detection is sold as a tool against plagiarism, ghost
writing, and gift authorship. Those are real harms. A detector is
not a moral solvent.

## What this repo is allowed to be

A personal revision kit. The documents are mine. The scores are
about those documents. If I show an examiner a hinge table, I am
showing a method, not an accusation.

## What I will not do with it

I will not run kerf on a classmate's unseen essay and treat a
flag as evidence. Short functional prose (lab notes, by-laws,
commit messages) has unstable rates. False changes are ordinary.
The cost of a false accusation is not symmetric with the cost of
a missed one, and I do not get to pick the cost after I like the
plot.

I will not scrape a forum or a workplace inbox to "enrich" the
bank. The whole point of writing twenty-eight files by hand is
that I know the consent situation: I consented.

## Dual use, said plainly

The same hinge list that could flag a gift paragraph in a paper
could be used to hunt for a whistle-blower's edits in a leaked
memo. I do not add features that make the second use easier
(author clustering with names, cross-document attribution, anything
that wants a real person's other writing).

## Shared-task data

If I ever touch official PAN dumps I will follow their terms and
I will not republish the text. This repo does not contain those
dumps and does not need them.

## The sentence I will end on

A method can be interesting and still be the wrong object to
point at a person.
