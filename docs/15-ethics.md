# Ethics

A hinge score is a **flag**, not a verdict. I built this lab to
rehearse an exam, not to accuse anyone of anything.

## What I will not do with it

- Run it on a classmate's take-home and announce that they "had
  help."
- Run it on a colleague's draft and imply gift authorship.
- Run it on a stranger's blog as a party trick that names them.
- Pretend a `1` on a short paragraph is evidence that would survive
  a conduct process.

False positives are cheap to produce and expensive for the person on
the other side of the table. Topic shifts, register shifts (a person
writing minutes and then a letter), and bilingual habits will all
move the score.

## What the toy bank is allowed to be

The gift-abstract file and the two-exam-answers file are **scenes I
wrote**. They are not real student submissions. I use them to
practise the *language* of the problem: inserted house, two answers
to one prompt. I do not use them as a detector for live coursework.

## Dual use, said plainly

Style-change methods are discussed in the literature for plagiarism
and for forensic linguistics. Those are real research areas with
their own ethics boards and, in forensic settings, their own rules
of evidence. A weekend zlib script is not that. I will not offer it
as if it were.

## Data

I did not put PAN Reddit threads in this repository. I did not scrape
anyone. The documents are mine. If I ever added a third-party corpus
I would keep it out of git and I would follow that corpus's licence.

## A sentence I want available in the oral

> I can explain a flag. I cannot tell you who wrote a paragraph, and
> I will not treat a flag as a name.
