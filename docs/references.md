# References (short, exam-useful)

These are pointers, not a reading list you will finish tonight.

- **PAN style-change detection** shared tasks (2017–). Paragraph-level
  change lists, later multi-author documents. This lab copies the
  *shape* of the I/O, not the data.
- **Burrows, J. (2002).** "Delta": a measure of stylistic difference
  and a guide to likely authorship. *Literary and Linguistic
  Computing.* Classical extrinsic Delta. Ours is intra-document only.
- **Mosteller & Wallace.** *Federalist* function-word study. The
  ancestor of "closed class first."
- **Cusum controversy** (Farringdon and critics). Useful as a story
  about a method that looks like science and is not a detector you
  should submit. We keep a CUSUM sketch for that reason.
- **Register** (Halliday / Biber). Field, tenor, mode. Our house
  voices are a cartoon of tenor.

If the examiner wants a citation, Burrows plus "PAN style-change
detection" is enough. Do not invent a paper you have not seen.
