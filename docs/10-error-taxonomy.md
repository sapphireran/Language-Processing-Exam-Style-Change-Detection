# Error taxonomy

Name the miss. A nameless error is just a wrong bit.

## `register_only`

The writers differ in person or hedge density, and the blender saw it. Not a miss.

Typical hits: problem-08, problem-09, problem-10, problem-22, problem-25.

## `topic_confound`

The detector snapped because the nouns moved. Gold is hold.

The River and Quill controls are the test. If they ever light up, a topic leak is back.

## `chat_wiggle`

Spark talking to Spark. Lowercase starts, questions, topic hops inside one chat. Problem-21 is the standing example. Gold is all holds; the default cut still false-alarms. Mention it before the examiner does.

## `same_register_miss`

Two cautious scientists, two planners, two callers. Topic held, person held, leftover function-word flavour too thin. Problem-13 is the flagship. Pred all zeros, gold one 1. Accuracy looks fine. Change-F1 is 0.

## `boundary_smear`

The true hinge fires, and so do the neighbours. Vinyl, amber, compost, miso-chat. Recall is 1, precision is not. Usually a leftover digit/semicolon echo inside Marble, or Spark staying loud after the snap.

## `return_blind`

The array marks two snaps but cannot say the third span is the first writer. Not a detector miss — a *label* limit. Problem-22: `authors=2`, `1+sum(changes)=3`.

## `gift_catch` / `paste_miss`

Gift (problem-25) is a student I-voice against a we-present abstract: register_only, currently a hit.

Paste (problem-26) is two exam answers on CUSUM. Both are careful, one slightly more hedged. Currently a miss. Good oral contrast: not every concatenation is loud.
