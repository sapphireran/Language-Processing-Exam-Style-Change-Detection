# Failure modes (say these before you are asked)

## Short paragraphs

A legal clause or a Slack line is 12–20 tokens. Function-word
histograms are mostly zeros. Character 3-grams still fire, which is
why the collage document is easy *for the wrong reason* (register is
cartoonish). Two short academic sentences would not be.

## Two similar academics

`04_hard_circadian` is the stress test in this repo. If I swapped
every `we` for `one` and deleted the hedges, the ensemble would
probably miss the cuts. That is also the honest PAN-hard story.

## Thresholds fit to the kit

`THRESHOLDS` in `detect.py` were not learned on PAN. They were
nudged until the fourteen study texts behaved. Recite that sentence.

## CUSUM on alternation

CUSUM loves a single late change (bikes then vellum). It is weaker
on A-B-A-B because the document mean sits between the two voices and
the path zigzags. That is why CUSUM is one vote, not the system.

## Topic leakage we did *not* fully remove

Content words are excluded from the dense vector, but character
3-grams still see `bike` and `vellum`. On the easy document that is
a feature. On a hard document with different rare technical terms
per author, 3-grams become a topic channel again. Mention it.

## Returning authors

We emit bits. We do not re-attach paragraph 3 to paragraph 1. The
ferry document is there so you remember the gap.

## Cleaning

PAN stripped markdown, URLs, and emoji. This kit's examples are
already clean. A real pipeline that forgets to strip `**bold**` or
`u/username` will detect formatting, not authors.

## Language

Everything here is English. Function-word lists do not travel. A
Danish or Chinese answer needs a new closed-class inventory and a
new story about tokenisation.

## Neural overconfidence

If you fine-tune on easy Reddit, you will screenshot a high F1 and
fail the hard split. Always report three numbers.
