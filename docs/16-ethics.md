# Ethics

This is a personal exam lab. It is not a forensic toolkit.

## What the files are

Every document in `examples/corpus/` is fiction I wrote in house voices. There is no student essay, no workplace email, no shared-task post. Gift-authorship and exam-paste files are staged teaching objects.

## What stylometry cannot do here

- Identify a person.
- Prove plagiarism on a six-sentence paragraph.
- Survive a register change by the *same* writer (tired, editing, quoting).

Short units make every number fragile. A hinge score is a study signal, not a verdict.

## What not to do with the code

- Do not score a classmate's work to "catch" them.
- Do not score employees, partners, or anonymous posters.
- Do not ship the default threshold as if it were calibrated on real forensic data.

## What to say if asked

> Intrinsic style-change detection can flag a register seam. On short text it is weak evidence. I would not accuse a person with this lab, and I did not build it on anyone's private writing.
