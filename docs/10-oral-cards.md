# Oral cards

Short enough to say. Mean enough to stop a follow-up.

## Task

"Given a document, label every hinge between consecutive
units as same-hand or new-hand. Intrinsic: no comparison
authors. Output length is units minus one."

## Thesis

"A style change is a bundle of closed-class isoglosses, not a
blended distance and not a topic jump."

## Features

"Person, contractions, deontics, hedges, formal versus oral
connectives, articles, digits, shape. Nouns never enter."

## Formula

"z is the adjacent jump over the document's own standard
deviation. A channel marks if it is loud and near its own
peak. A hinge fires if at least three channels mark."

## Why k=3

"One mark is a wrinkle. Two is a coincidence I will not
swear to. Three is a dialect border I can defend."

## Topic

"I can rotate the craft and keep the house. That file must
not fire. I can keep the craft and change the house. That
file must fire. Those two sentences are the hard band."

## Metrics

"This bank is mostly zeros. Never-fire is accurate and empty.
I quote macro-F1 and I put the two liars on the same table."

## Why not a transformer

"I can starve a feature list. I cannot starve an encoder in
ten minutes. If the examiner wants neural, I will say it
should be trained on function-word masks, and I will still
start with this atlas."

## Failure I will admit

"Roll versus Brine is the near pair: both deontic, both
third person. If I miss, I missed *was* versus *shall*, not
the granite."

## Ethics

"A lab score is not a verdict about a person. I will not
talk about this tool as if it named a ghostwriter."

## Holdout

"I did not tune on 04, 10, 16, 22, 26, 30. If those are
dirty, the story is dirty."
